Chnoue Wing — next 9 days

How does the water in La Ch'noue work? La Ch'noue is a marsh fed by the Rocade lock. When the lock does an INTAKE (PRISE), it fills the marsh at high tide (up to ~5.20 m in summer), then holds the water for several days → you can ride. When it does a DRAIN (RENVOI) or a low-tide closure, it empties the marsh → not enough water. On tidal days, the level rises and falls with the tide. In short: marsh full = go, marsh drained = wait for the next intake.

Frequently asked questions about La Ch'noue

Where is La Ch'noue?

La Ch'noue (also called the "chenal des Sables") is a channel of the salt marsh in Les Sables-d'Olonne, in the Vendée (France): a flat-water spot ideal for wingfoil, wing surf, windsurf and windfoil, and perfect for learning. Access is on the route de l'Aubraie / Salines watersports base side, via the Gymnase des Sauniers car park.

Which watersports can you do at La Ch'noue?

The flat water of La Ch'noue is great for wingfoil (wing foil), wing surf, windsurf and windfoil. For surf in the waves, head to the ocean side, on the Les Sables-d'Olonne beaches (Tanchet, la Grande Plage, l'Aubraie).

When can you ride wingfoil or windsurf at La Ch'noue?

When the marsh is full (Rocade lock on INTAKE or water held) and there is wind: from ~10-12 knots on a big wing, ~15 knots on a small wing or windsurf. This site works out these "GO" windows over 9 days from the wind, the tides and the lock calendar.

Why is La Ch'noue sometimes dry?

The level depends on the marsh locks (mainly the Rocade lock): they fill the marsh (INTAKE) and hold it for several days, or empty it (DRAIN / low-tide closure). Drained marsh = not enough water to foil until the next intake.

What gear for La Ch'noue?

A foil mast of about 80 cm clears the bottom when the marsh is full; windsurf and wing surf work well on this flat water too. The most favourable wind directions are West, North-West and North.
