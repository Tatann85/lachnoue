Frequently asked questions about La Ch'noue

Where is La Ch'noue?

La Ch'noue is a channel of the salt marsh in Les Sables-d'Olonne (Vendée, France): a flat-water spot ideal for wingfoil, windfoil and learning. Access is on the route de l'Aubraie / Salines watersports base side.

When can you ride wingfoil at La Ch'noue?

When the marsh is full (Rocade lock on INTAKE or water held) and there is wind: from ~10-12 knots on a big wing, ~15 knots on a small wing.

Why is La Ch'noue sometimes dry?

The level depends on the Rocade lock: it fills the marsh (INTAKE) and holds it for several days, or empties it (DRAIN / low-tide closure). Drained marsh = not enough water to foil until the next intake.

What gear for La Ch'noue?

A foil mast of about 80 cm clears the bottom when the marsh is full. The most favourable wind directions are West, North-West and North (flat water).
